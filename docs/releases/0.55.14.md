# 0.55.14

## Active Camera source-stage decomposition and contract correction

The complete 0.55.13 Blender acceptance passed for Perspective and Orthographic Active Camera projection with zero camera-depth delta. The subsequent full Python suite exposed one real architecture regression and two stale append/order contracts.

### Changed

- `prepare_a1_source_geometry()` is decomposed into explicit request, world-normalization, projection-route, geometry-completion, statistics, and logging owners.
- Source UV boundary ownership is validated immediately after world-transform normalization for both signed-axis and Active Camera routes.
- Signed-axis geometry remains prepared after projected Z-group assignment.
- Active Camera segmentation, decomposition, and strict triangulation remain in normalized world geometry before nonlinear camera projection.

### Fixed

- The source-stage architecture gate now verifies every explicit owner remains below the monolith threshold and that the public stage calls them in deterministic order.
- The world-transform ordering contract is route-aware instead of assuming one global geometry ordering for both linear signed-axis and nonlinear Active Camera projection.
- The append-only `A1DocumentAssemblySettings` contract now includes `compensate_depth_setup_y` after the existing UV range fields.

### Unchanged

- Active Camera projection matrices, pre-projection triangulation, camera-depth math, screen placement, nearest-vertex object-block order, rig assembly, and attachment compensation are unchanged.
- The strict polygon planarity and camera-depth tolerances remain unchanged.
- Existing rendered Camera Projection, connected and mixed assembly, Spine codecs, texture baking, and public UI remain unchanged.
